## Choose a booklet

- **Difficulty:** `level-1` through `level-10`.
- **Technique practice:** separate categories such as Hidden Single, X-Wing, Swordfish and XY-Chain.
- **Layout and extras:** use the PDF filename to choose the number of puzzles per page, puzzle-only editions, answers and candidate-note editions.
- **Paper:** the inspected booklet covers specify A4 / US Letter.

The original booklets include their own printing, sharing and solving-support notes. Keep those pages with the PDFs.
